A PUBLIC LINK NEEDS REAL BOUNDARIES

Care is part of the product.

Report, then review.

A reported conversation is hidden before review. The server attempts operator email and push alerts immediately, with durable retries if delivery fails.

Block means both ways.

Blocking stops contact for both accounts. It has no expiry or unblock option. It cannot erase contact information already copied outside ORBIT.

A real limit.

The server admits no more than 100 non-deleted account records, including staff and suspended accounts. At capacity, a verified-email waitlist offers no promised date or reserved place.

ORBIT is not an emergency service. In immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Admission stays closed until moderation coverage and release checks are ready.
