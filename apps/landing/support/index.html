Report a safety concern

In ORBIT, open the introduction → Safety center → describe what happened → Send report. The reported conversation is hidden before review; email and push alerts are attempted immediately for configured operators. To stop contact now, choose Block this person and confirm the permanent, bidirectional block.

Delete an account

  1. Sign in to your ORBIT account.
  2. Open Settings → Delete account.
  3. Read and confirm the seven-day cancellation period.
  4. After the deadline, the worker removes the account and associated personal data. Limited safety/legal records may remain where necessary.

Settings → Create signed export lets you download your data first. If you cannot sign in, a private support mailbox must be available to verify ownership; no anonymous deletion form is offered here. This page is not proof that an outside-app deletion request has been processed.

Leave the waitlist

Verify your email through sign-in. At capacity or while admissions are paused, join or remove your waitlist entry on the waitlist screen. A waitlist entry is separate from an account. There are no automatically sent invitations or marketing messages.

Immediate danger
